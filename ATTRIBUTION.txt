Block models (Models\Cubes\Large\*, Models\Cubes\Small\*) and six
textures (Dome_Glass_cm, Dampener_cm, TacScreen_cm, TacScreen_2_cm,
rgby_add, rgby_ng)

By MTGraves, from two of his packs:
  Naval Theme Prop Pack
  https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2494017078
  Comms pack
  https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3504671538

The textures are here because the meshes reference them by path. Without
them a subscriber who does not own those packs would see untextured blocks -
and it would look correct to anyone testing with the packs installed.

Used with his explicit permission, granted 2026-08-09. His words: "thank you
for asking first, and good luck!"

The models are redistributed unmodified. The block definitions around them
are original; the Rotating Antenna definition is adapted from his.

Subpart rotation code derives from Digi's public spinning-subpart example,
by way of the same pack.
